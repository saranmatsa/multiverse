# Failed Approaches & Dead Ends (Honest Log)

This document records approaches that were considered, attempted, or found in literature but failed to constitute proof.

---

## 1. CMB Bubble Collision Searches
**Approach**: Look for circular temperature discontinuities in CMB from bubble nucleation
**Groups**: Feeney et al. (2011), Planck Collaboration (2013, 2015)
**Result**: No significant detections. Upper limits on collision rate.
**Why it fails as proof**: Null result constrains parameter space but doesn't prove existence. Absence of evidence ≠ evidence of absence.

---

## 2. "Quantum Computing Proves Many Worlds"
**Approach**: Deutsch's argument that quantum computing exploits parallel branches
**Reality**: Quantum algorithms work in ALL interpretations (Copenhagen, Bohm, QBism, MWI)
**Why it fails**: Category error - empirical equivalence of interpretations. Computation doesn't discriminate.

---

## 3. "Fine-Tuning Proves Multiverse"
**Approach**: Anthropic argument - multiverse explains fine-tuned constants
**Problems**:
- Requires measure to compute "typicality" - no measure = no prediction
- Post-diction, not prediction (we already know constants)
- Alternative explanations: unknown physical principle, simulation, design
- Inverse gambler's fallacy debate (Hacking, White, Bradley)

---

## 4. "Mathematical Effectiveness Proves Level IV"
**Approach**: Wigner's "unreasonable effectiveness" → reality IS mathematics
**Problems**:
- Map ≠ territory (Korzybski)
- Evolutionary explanation: we developed math to model world
- Selection bias: we only notice successful math
- Category error: language vs reality

---

## 5. "Inflation Proves Multiverse"
**Approach**: Inflation → eternal inflation → multiverse
**Problems**:
- Inflation not proven (alternatives: cyclic, bounce, string gas)
- Eternal inflation generic but not inevitable (depends on potential)
- Measure problem: eternal inflation + no measure = no predictions
- BGV theorem: inflation past-incomplete → initial conditions still needed

---

## 6. "String Theory Landscape Proves Level II"
**Approach**: String theory → 10^500 vacua → multiverse
**Problems**:
- Landscape not derived; KKLT construction challenged
- Swampland conjectures: de Sitter may not exist in string theory
- No Standard Model vacuum found
- Measure problem on landscape unsolved

---

## 7. "Born Rule Derivation Proves MWI"
**Approach**: Decision theory (Deutsch-Wallace), envariance (Zurek), self-locating uncertainty (Carroll-Sebens)
**Problems**:
- Circular: assumes probabilistic structure to derive probability
- Incoherence problem: if all outcomes happen, "probability" meaningless
- Multiple incompatible derivations → no consensus
- Critics: Lewis, Albert, Vaidman, Price, Kent

---

## 8. "Wigner's Friend / Frauchiger-Renner Proves MWI"
**Approach**: Extended Wigner's friend paradoxes show single-world interpretations inconsistent
**Reality**: 
- Tests *single-world* vs *no-collapse* interpretations
- Doesn't distinguish MWI from other no-collapse views (Bohm, QBism, relational)
- Experimental tests (Proietti et al. 2019) confirm QM predictions, not MWI specifically

---

## 9. "Decoherence Proves Branching"
**Approach**: Decoherence explains appearance of collapse → branching real
**Problems**:
- Decoherence is *approximate*, not fundamental
- Doesn't solve preferred basis problem (environment selects, but why this environment?)
- Compatible with Copenhagen (decoherence + collapse), Bohm, MWI
- Doesn't derive Born rule

---

## 10. "Simulating Multiverse Proves It"
**Approach**: If we can simulate it, it exists (Bostrom, Tegmark)
**Problems**:
- Simulation ≠ reality (Searle's Chinese Room)
- Computational universe hypothesis untestable
- Infinite regress: what simulates the simulator?
- Category error: representation vs represented

---

## Summary: Why All "Proofs" Fail

| Approach | Fatal Flaw |
|----------|------------|
| CMB searches | Null results; not proof of existence |
| Quantum computing | Empirical equivalence of interpretations |
| Fine-tuning | Post-diction; measure problem |
| Math effectiveness | Map/territory confusion |
| Inflation → multiverse | Inflation unproven; measure problem |
| String landscape | Unverified; swampland challenges |
| Born rule derivations | Circular; contested |
| Wigner's friend | Tests collapse vs no-collapse, not MWI specifically |
| Decoherence | Approximate; interpretation-neutral |
| Simulation argument | Untestable metaphysics |

---

## Lesson

**Every proposed "proof" of the multiverse either:**
1. Confuses evidence for a *precursor theory* (inflation) with evidence for the multiverse itself
2. Commits a category error (interpretation vs theory, metaphysics vs physics)
3. Assumes a measure that doesn't exist
4. Produces post-dictions, not predictions
5. Is empirically equivalent to non-multiverse alternatives

**This is not a failure of imagination - it's a structural feature of the hypotheses themselves.**
