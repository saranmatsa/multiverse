## Categories of Multiverse Theories

1. **Level I**: Regions beyond our cosmological horizon (same physics, different initial conditions)
2. **Level II**: Bubble universes from eternal inflation (different physical constants)
3. **Level III**: Many-worlds interpretation of quantum mechanics (all outcomes realized)
4. **Level IV**: Ultimate ensemble (all mathematical structures exist) - Tegmark
