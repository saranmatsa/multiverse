# Level III Multiverse: Many-Worlds Interpretation of Quantum Mechanics

## Theory
All outcomes of quantum measurements are realized in different branches of the universal wavefunction. No collapse postulate - unitary evolution always. Schrödinger equation applies to everything including observers.

## Origin
- **Everett (1957)**: "Relative State" formulation - PhD thesis under Wheeler
- **DeWitt (1970)**: Popularized as "many-worlds"
- **Deutsch, Wallace, Saunders, Vaidman, Carroll**: Modern developments

## Evidence FOR
- **Solves measurement problem**: No ad hoc collapse, no Heisenberg cut
- **Only interpretation** that takes Schrödinger equation literally at all scales
- **Derives Born rule** from decision theory (Deutsch-Wallace) or envariance (Zurek) or self-locating uncertainty (Carroll-Sebens)
- **Explains quantum computing**: Parallel computation across branches
- **Consistent with decoherence theory**: Branching = decoherence (Zurek, Joos, Zeh)

## Evidence AGAINST / Major Problems

### 1. Probability Problem (CRITICAL)
- **Incoherence problem**: If all outcomes happen, what does "probability" mean?
- **Born rule derivation**: Controversial - circular? (Lewis, Albert, Vaidman, Price critiques)
- **Self-locating uncertainty**: Carroll-Sebens approach - still debated

### 2. Preferred Basis Problem
- **Why THIS basis?** (position, not momentum; energy, not something else)
- **Decoherence answer**: Environment selects pointer basis (Zurek)
- **But**: Decoherence is approximate, not fundamental

### 3. Ontological Extravagance
- **Unobservable branches**: Infinite/unobservable realities
- **Occam's razor violation**: Postulates vast unobservable ontology
- **Counter**: "Ontological parsimony" - fewer postulates (no collapse)

### 4. Energy Conservation
- **Where does energy come from** for new branches?
- **Answer**: Energy conserved globally in universal wavefunction; not defined locally per branch

### 5. Relativity Compatibility
- **Preferred foliation?** Branching needs time slicing
- **Relativistic MWI**: Still under development (Wallace, Greaves)

### 6. Empirical Equivalence
- **No experiment distinguishes MWI from Copenhagen, Bohm, QBism, etc.**
- **All interpretations predict same Born rule statistics**

## Key Calculations

### Branching Rate
Decoherence time τ_D ~ ħ²/(2mkTγx²) for spatial superposition
- For dust grain (10⁻¹⁷ kg, 10⁻⁶ m) at 300K: τ_D ~ 10⁻³¹ s
- Branching extremely rapid for macroscopic objects

### Number of Branches
- Each quantum interaction with environment → branching
- Estimate: ~10^(10^120) branches since Big Bang (very rough)

### Quantum Computing
- N qubits → 2^N branches during computation
- 50 qubits → 10¹⁵ branches
- 300 qubits → 10⁹⁰ branches (exceeds atoms in observable universe)

## Falsifiability
- **Fundamentally unfalsifiable** - empirically equivalent to other interpretations
- **Proposed tests** (all contested):
  - Wigner's friend experiments (Frauchiger-Renner paradox)
  - Interference of macroscopic superpositions (testing collapse models)
  - But: These test *collapse models*, not MWI vs other no-collapse interpretations

## Status
**Respectable minority interpretation**. 
- ~10-20% of physicists (surveys vary)
- Popular in quantum foundations, quantum information
- **Not proven**, **not falsifiable**, **interpretation not theory**

## Critical Distinction
**MWI is an INTERPRETATION**, not a theory. It makes identical predictions to Copenhagen, Bohmian mechanics, QBism, etc. "Proving MWI" is a category error - you prove *theories*, not *interpretations*.
