# Level I Multiverse: Regions Beyond Cosmological Horizon

## Theory
Regions of space beyond our particle horizon (~46.5 Glyr). Same physical laws, same constants, different initial conditions. Predicted by standard cosmology + inflation.

## Evidence FOR
- **Flatness of universe** (Ω ≈ 1.00 ± 0.02 from Planck 2018) → inflation → generically eternal → Level I follows
- **Homogeneity/isotropy** on super-horizon scales requires explanation; inflation provides it and predicts regions beyond horizon
- **Standard ΛCDM cosmology** + inflation → mathematically implies regions we cannot see

## Evidence AGAINST / Caveats
- **Not directly observable** by definition (beyond particle horizon)
- **Inflation not proven** - alternative models exist (cyclic, bounce, string gas)
- **Measure problem**: How to count "typical" observers in infinite space?

## Key Calculations

### Particle Horizon Distance
```
d_p = c ∫_0^t_0 dt/a(t) ≈ 46.5 Glyr (comoving)
```

### Inflation Predicts Eternal Inflation
If inflation lasts > ~60 e-folds (solves flatness/horizon), quantum fluctuations dominate → eternal inflation → Level I regions

### Probability of Identical Hubble Volumes
In infinite space, probability → 1 that exact duplicates exist (finite configurations in finite volume)
- Estimated distance to nearest duplicate: ~10^(10^115) meters (Tegmark 2003)

## Falsifiability
- **Indirect**: Detect curvature (Ω ≠ 1) → rules out standard inflation → undermines Level I
- **Indirect**: Detect non-Gaussianity inconsistent with inflation
- **Direct**: Impossible by definition

## Status
**Mainstream cosmology**. Not controversial among cosmologists who accept inflation. The controversy is whether inflation itself is correct.
